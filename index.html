Zisen Shao 邵子森

I am a second-year master's student in Computer Science at the University of Maryland, College Park, where I conducted research under the guidance of Prof. Ruohan Gao, and collaborated with Prof. Dinesh Manocha.

Previously, I earned my bachelor's degree in Computer Science from the University of Wisconsin–Madison, where I worked with Prof. Josiah Hanna on Robot Vision at RoboCup.

I'm interested in computer vision and computer graphics. My research focuses on physics-informed models for multisensory digital twins. My ultimate goal is to build an interactive digital replica of real world that engages the full range of human senses.
